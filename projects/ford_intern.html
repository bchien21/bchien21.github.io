Cognex Camera OCR, VBA Automation for Excel Spreadsheets, and PLC Ladder Logic Programming for HMIs

Background

Technical achievements from an internship with the onsite Ford engineering team at the BlueOval SK Battery Park in Glendale, KY.

Optical Character Recognition and Image Classification on a Cognex Platform

  • Background: Cognex supplies software used to refine pre-trained edge-learning models for a specific task before they run on Cognex machine-vision cameras.
  • Accomplishment: Collected a small dataset of images, and fine-tuned these edge-learned models for a specific image classification and optical character recognition task. This demonstrates the potential for utilizing Cognex cameras in critical tasks such as defect detection.

Elevator Simulation with PLC Programming

  • Background: Most of the PLCs in the factory were Mitsubishi, and learning to program them will be a critical skill for onsite engineers.
  • Accomplishment: Used Mitsubishi's Gx Works (PLC) and GT Designer (HMI) software to design an HMI elevator simulation powered by ladder logic.

Automated Updating of Equipment Status Spreadsheets with VBA

  • Background: The Ford team maintained in-house spreadsheets to track the bring-up status of plant equipment freshly shipped from Korea.
  • Accomplishment: Developed VBA programs to automatically update these spreadsheets when given a report from a contracting firm overseeing the actual bring-up. The video above is one such example of automatic updating.
